The Chef

Aiko Tanaka has worked one counter for nineteen years.

Trained in Tsukiji before it moved, Aiko opened Shiro with a single rule: the person who buys the fish is the person who cuts it. There is no pass, no expediter — every plate crosses the same eleven feet of counter, from her hands to yours.

The menu is never printed in advance. It's decided at 6am at the dock, and it doesn't change once service starts.

Chef Aiko Tanaka, at the counter
