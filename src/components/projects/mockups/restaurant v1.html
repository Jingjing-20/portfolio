Since 2019

A quiet room, a loud fire.

Noir & Sel began as a single wood-fired hearth and a chalkboard menu of four dishes. The room has grown; the instinct hasn't. Everything that reaches the table has passed near an open flame or a cure — nothing hides behind sauce.

We work with a short list of growers, one fishmonger, and a butcher we've used since the first week we opened. The dining room seats forty-two. We keep it that way on purpose.
