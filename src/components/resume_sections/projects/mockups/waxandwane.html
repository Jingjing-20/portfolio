About

Two collectors who ran out of shelf space.

Wax & Wane started as a closet's worth of records and a shared conviction that most shops make you shop faster than you want to. We built the kind we'd want to stay in.

History

2016

Two friends start trading records out of a shared apartment, mostly soul and early electronic.

2019

The first storefront opens, one room, four crates, and a single pair of headphones behind the counter.

2022

The bar opens next door. Six stations, a coffee machine, and a wine list chosen by ear as much as taste.

Now

Weekly sessions, a growing local-press shelf, and a club that ships one record a month to a few hundred people.
