    About The Drift

    The Drift is written and edited by Priya Ostrander, a former city-desk reporter who got tired of covering news the day it happened and started writing about what it looked like a year later. New essays publish most Tuesdays and Fridays.

    Pitches and letters are read, though not always answered quickly — hello@thedrift.example.

    Publishing

    One long essay most Tuesdays, one shorter piece most Fridays. No fixed word count — a story runs as long as it needs to.

    Independence

    No ads, no sponsored posts. The Drift is funded entirely by reader subscriptions.

    Pitching

    Send a paragraph, not a full draft. Reported essays and personal ones are both welcome — no breaking news.

    "I'm not interested in what happened this morning. I'm interested in what's still true about it a year from now."

    — Priya Ostrander, founding editor
